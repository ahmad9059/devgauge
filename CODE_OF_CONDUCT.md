# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DevGauge welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Participation should be respectful, inclusive, and focused on improving the project.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in a public community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and assume good intent where reasonable.
- Offer specific, constructive feedback about ideas and implementations.
- Accept feedback professionally and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.
- Keep conversations relevant and help make participation accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, or repeated disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials or sensitive account data belonging to others.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.

## Reporting concerns

Contact the repository maintainer through the contact options on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/ahmad9059). If no private contact option is available, request a private reporting channel without posting the incident details or anyone's personal information publicly.

When reporting privately, provide relevant links, a description of the behavior, and enough context to understand the concern. Share only information necessary to investigate. Security vulnerabilities should follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit inappropriate content, restrict interactions, close disruptive discussions, or temporarily or permanently exclude participants from project spaces.

Responses are based on the nature, severity, and recurrence of the behavior. A minor incident may receive a request to change behavior or a warning. Serious or repeated violations may result in an immediate restriction or ban.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Information may be shared with people who need it to investigate or enforce this policy. Maintainers involved in a reported incident should not be the sole decision-maker for that report when another maintainer is available.

## Questions

For questions about this policy, contact the maintainer using the same reporting route. Raise policy improvements respectfully and avoid exposing confidential incidents in public discussion.
